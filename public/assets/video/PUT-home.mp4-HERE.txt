Put your hero video in this folder, named exactly:  home.mp4

It plays automatically behind the homepage hero (muted, looping, with Sound on / Pause buttons).
No code changes needed: refresh the page after adding or replacing the file.

Tips for a fast, reliable video
- Format: MP4 with H.264 video (plays in every browser). H.265/HEVC (some phone exports)
  does not play in all browsers.
- Size: aim for under ~8 MB; 1080p or smaller; 10–30 seconds that loop cleanly.
- To convert/compress any clip:
  ffmpeg -i input.mov -vf scale=-2:1080 -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 26 -movflags +faststart -c:a aac -b:a 128k home.mp4

If the file is missing or can't play, the homepage shows the city skyline instead.
